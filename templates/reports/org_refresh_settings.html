Auto-refresh

Scheduled reports rebuild on their cron whether or not anyone still opens them, which costs warehouse time and worker capacity. When a report has gone this many days without a view, its scheduled refresh is suspended: the schedule stays in report.yaml, but it no longer fires. Reports with an enabled email schedule or schedule-triggered alert are never suspended, since the people receiving those never open the portal. A suspended report is resumed manually by a developer with Resume auto-refresh, which also queues an immediate rebuild.
