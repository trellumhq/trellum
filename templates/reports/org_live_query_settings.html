Live queries

Most reports are compiled: the data is baked into the build and the page just draws it. A live report is the exception — a few of its components run a query against your warehouse when someone opens or filters it, so viewers see current, per-entity detail that would be too large to bake. Whether a report is live is a build-time choice by whoever authors it; this page governs, for the whole organization, how hard those live lookups may run.
